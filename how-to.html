上大人

The concept of it is to create ORDER out of CHAOS based on random drawing of tiles (cards).
其理念是通过随机摸牌（抽牌），在混乱中建立秩序。
-- Julia Roberts (on Mahjong, a concept shared by Shangdaren) -- 朱莉娅·罗伯茨 (关于麻将的见解，上大人亦有异曲同工之妙)

How to Play Shangdaren

上大人玩法说明

Shangdaren is a traditional literacy card game. It requires 3 players and a specialized deck of characters.

上大人是一种传统的识字纸牌游戏。它需要 3 名玩家和一套特殊的字符牌阵。

1. The Cards

1. 游戏纸牌 (The Cards)

The deck consists of 96 cards: 24 unique characters, with 4 copies each. These are organized into 8 suits of 3 characters.

  • Meat Cards (荤牌): The first 3 suits (High Value).
  • Veggie Cards (素牌): The remaining 5 suits (Lower Value).
  • Colors: Odd suits are Red, even suits are Black (mainly for grouping).

整副牌共有 96 张：24 种不同的汉字，每种 4 张拷贝。它们被分为 8 个系列（Suit），每个系列包含 3 个汉字。

  • 荤牌 (Meat): 前 3 个系列（分值较高）。
  • 素牌 (Veggie): 剩下的 5 个系列（分值较低）。
  • 颜色: 奇数系列为 红色，偶数系列为 黑色（主要用于视觉分组）。

2. Game Setup & Flow

2. 游戏设置与流程

  • Initial Deal: Each player starts with 15 private cards (HAND).
  • Turns: Play proceeds counter-clockwise.
  • Drawing: A player starts their turn by drawing one card from the deck.
  • Actions: Once a card is drawn, players can claim it to form a Meld.
  • Discarding: After taking a card to form a meld, you must discard an unneeded card to keep 15 cards in play.
  • 初始发牌: 每位玩家开始时拥有 15 张暗牌 (HAND)。
  • 牌权切换: 游戏按逆时针进行。
  • 摸牌: 玩家在自己的回合从公牌堆中摸一张牌。
  • 动作 (Actions): 牌摸出后，符合条件的玩家可以执行动作来组成 牌组 (Meld)。
  • 打牌: 如果执行了动作或摸牌后未和牌，必须打出一张不需要的牌，以维持 15 张在场牌的状态。

3. Valid Melds

3. 有效牌组 (Meld)

Melds help organize your hand into structured sets to earn points and win.

牌组可以帮助你将杂乱的暗牌整理成有序的组合，部分牌组可得福分并助你“和”牌。

Meld Formation & Upgrades

牌组形成与升级规则

  • Exposed Priority: When an incoming card can form or grow a meld in both your exposed area and your hand, priority is given to forming or upgrading the exposed meld first (e.g., if you have an 尔小 KOU in both exposed and hand, an incoming 生 upgrades the exposed KOU to CHI first).
  • Same-suit order: Inside one suit, form a 4-of-a-kind pattern first (two dragons, then a full dragon/snake, then a half dragon/snake, then a plain quad), then a 3-of-a-kind, then CHI, then KOU. A 3-of-a-kind or 4-of-a-kind is never split into CHI or KOU. If you drew the completing card, the group is SAO, KAI-ZAO, DRAGON, or HALF-DRAGON. If another player drew or passed it, the group is PENG, XIA-CE, SNAKE, or HALF-SNAKE.
  • Grow or Merge Only (Never Split): Melds in the exposed area can grow or merge, but can never be split:
    • KOU → CHI: An exposed KOU grows into a CHI upon claiming the missing third character of the suit.
    • 3-of-a-Kind / SAO Upgrades: A 3-of-a-kind in hand or an exposed SAO (self-drawn 3-of-a-kind) grows when the 4th identical card arrives into KAI-ZAO / XIA-CE (if no same-suit cards are attached); into HALF-DRAGON / HALF-SNAKE if you already hold 1 other card of the same suit in hand at that moment (this extra card cannot come from a future draw or another player); or into a full DRAGON / SNAKE if you hold the other 2 cards of the same suit in hand or in exposed melds.
    • HALF-DRAGON / HALF-SNAKE → TWO-DRAGONS-TUSSLING (双龙抱柱): Grows when a second 4-of-a-kind from the same suit is formed (under standard quad rules) alongside the 1 extra card of that suit from your hand.
    • PENG Cannot Grow: An exposed PENG meld cannot be further grown or reformed.
  • 明牌优先: 当一张来牌既能与明牌区的牌组结合，也能与手牌（暗牌）结合时，优先形成或升级明牌区的牌组（例如：若明牌区与手牌中都有“尔小”口，摸到或接“生”时优先将明牌区的口升级为吃）。
  • 同系列先后: 同一系列里，先组四张类牌组（双龙抱柱，然后龙/蛇，然后半龙/半蛇，然后普通四张），再组三张，然后吃，最后口。三张或四张不能拆开去凑吃或口。自己摸到的那张按绍、开招、龙、半龙计；别人摸出或搬出的那张按碰、下扯、蛇、半蛇计。
  • 只合不拆（明牌升级与合并）: 明牌区的牌组可以继续生长或合并，但绝不能拆分：
    • 口 → 吃: 明牌区的“口”在获得同系列第三张牌后升级为“吃”。
    • 三张 / 绍的升级: 手牌中的三张相同牌或明牌区的“绍”（自摸三张）在遇到第四张相同牌时，可升级为开招 / 下扯；若此时手牌中已有同系列的另外 1 张牌，则升级为半龙 / 半蛇（该额外单牌不能来自后续摸牌或他人之牌）；若在手牌或明牌中已有同系列的另外 2 张牌，则可合并升级为完整的龙 / 蛇。
    • 半龙 / 半蛇 → 双龙抱柱: 当同系列的第二组四张按正常四张规则成型，加上手牌中同系列的另外 1 张牌时，可升级为双龙抱柱 (TWO-DRAGONS-TUSSLING)。
    • 碰不可再变: 明牌区的“碰”不能再继续升级或重组。

4. Must-Follow Rules

4. 强制执行规则 (Must-Follow)

Certain actions are mandatory if you possess the required cards:

当满足以下条件时，玩家必须执行相应动作：

If a card completes a 3-of-a-kind (PENG/SAO) or 4-of-a-kind (XIA-CE/KAI-ZAO), you MUST claim it.
如果摸出的牌能组成三张（碰/绍）或四张（下扯/开招），你必须声明并接牌。
If you draw a card, or the previous player passes a card, and it can form a "CHI" for you, you MUST claim it.
如果你摸牌或上家搬牌后能组成“吃 (CHI)”，你必须接牌执行动作。
If a card lets you declare HARMONY and it was self-drawn or drawn/passed by your upstream player (right), you MUST take it. If your downstream player (left) drew it, Harmony is optional and you may decline.
若该牌可让你宣布“和”，且由你自己摸到或由上家（右侧）摸出/搬出，则必须接牌。若由下家（左侧）摸出，则可选择放弃（例如争取自摸加分）。
No do-over: after you discard any card of a suit, pass a card of that suit you drew, or your upstream player has already passed a card of that suit, you cannot form KOU in that suit for the rest of the game. The card your upstream player is passing to you right now still may be used for KOU. If that card is the last one you need, HARMONY stays available and KOU does not.
不能回头组口：本局中只要打出过某系列的任意一张牌，自己搬过该系列中自己摸到的牌，或上家已经搬过该系列的牌，就不能再以该系列组口。上家此刻正搬给你的这一张仍可以组口。若这张牌正好是和牌所缺的最后一张，仍可以宣布和，但不会出现口。
A KOU cannot take a card out of an in-hand CHI, or out of an in-hand 3-of-a-kind or 4-of-a-kind. A spare copy that the CHI does not need may still form a KOU.
组口不能拆掉手里已经成套的吃，也不能拆手里的三张或四张。吃用不到的多余一张仍可以组口。
Naked 4-of-a-kind in hand: when you hold a 4-of-a-kind in hand with no other cards from the same suit, the first time you are required to discard a card, you must reveal the 4-of-a-kind in the exposed area and skip that discard (since every naked 4-of-a-kind requires one extra card to reach Harmony).
暗牌净四张亮出：当手牌中持有四张相同的牌且没有同系列的其它牌时，在首次需要打牌时，必须将该四张亮到明牌区并跳过本次打牌（因为每有一组净四张，和牌时都需要多凑一张牌）。

5. Scoring & Harmony

5. 计分与和 (Harmony)

To win (declare HARMONY / 和), you must satisfy these conditions:

要宣布胜利（和/HARMONY），必须满足以下条件：

  1. Reach at least 10 points (Fu/福). An all-veggie hand (素牌) is exempt, unless it contains a SAO or any meld with a 4-of-a-kind (XIA-CE, KAI-ZAO, DRAGON, SNAKE, HALF-DRAGON, HALF-SNAKE, or TWO-DRAGONS-TUSSLING), in hand or exposed. PENG, CHI, and KOU do not remove the exemption.
  2. Form a total of 16 cards into valid melds (15 in hand + 1 completing card, plus 1 extra card for each exposed quad). Follow the same-suit order above. On the reveal, an exposed PENG stays PENG. A 3-of-a-kind completed by another player's card is PENG. Only a 3-of-a-kind that was still concealed in hand is shown as SAO.
  3. Have exactly 2 "KOU" combinations (note: Half-Dragon/Snake counts as 1 KOU).
  4. Score at least 1 reward (大). Bonuses stack. A hand that meets every other Harmony condition but scores 0 rewards cannot win and must keep playing. If a mandatory meld then leaves no legal discard, that player is baozi.
  1. 得分达到至少 10 福 (Fu)。若和牌组合全部为素牌，则不受此限制；但如果其中有绍，或任何含四张相同牌的牌组（下扯、开招、龙、蛇、半龙、半蛇、双龙抱柱），无论在暗牌还是明牌，仍须满 10 福。碰、吃、口不取消这项豁免。
  2. 全部 16 张牌（15张在场+1张摸/接牌，每组已亮出的四张再加 1 张）必须全部组成有效牌组，并遵守上面的同系列先后顺序。亮牌时，已经碰出的牌仍是碰；别人打出的那张凑成的三张也是碰。只有原本藏在手里的三张才标成绍。
  3. 必须包含且仅包含 2 个“口” (KOU)（注：半蛇或半龙会被计为 1 个“口”）。
  4. 至少获得 1 个“大”。各项奖励可以累加。若其它和牌条件都已满足但“大”为 0，则不能和，必须继续打。若此时强制成牌后没有合法的牌可打，该玩家成为包子。
